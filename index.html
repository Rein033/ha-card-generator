What can HA Card Generator do?

    
Build Home Assistant dashboard cards with a form, see a live preview in light and dark, and copy the YAML. Tile, Entities, Gauge, Mushroom, button-card, stacks and grids.

    
  • Built-in cards: Tile with features, Entities, Button, Glance, Gauge, Thermostat, Light, Weather, Markdown, Heading
  • Vertical stack, horizontal stack and grid, as deep as you like
  • Community cards: Mushroom, button-card and Universal 3D Cards
  • Shows which HACS cards you need, with a one-click Open in HACS link
  • Examples to start from, paste existing YAML to edit it, share a design with a link

  

  
Frequently asked questions
How do I add the card to my dashboard?
Open your dashboard, click Edit, add a card, choose Manual at the bottom of the list, paste the YAML and save.
Do I need HACS?
Only for community cards (types starting with custom:). The box above the YAML shows exactly which ones you need and links straight to them in HACS.
Can I edit a card I already have?
Yes. Click Paste YAML, paste your card and it loads into the form, including stacks and grids.
Does it work with my theme?
The preview uses the default Home Assistant look in light and dark. In your dashboard the card follows your own theme; you can design one with the HA Theme Generator.
